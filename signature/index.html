Preview

If the copy button doesn't work in your browser, click inside the white preview, press Cmd+A (or Ctrl+A) then Cmd+C, and paste into Gmail.

Add it to Gmail

Six steps, once.

  1. Above, fill in your details and click Copy signature.
  2. In Gmail on the web, click the gear icon at the top right, then See all settings.
  3. Stay on the General tab and scroll to Signature. Click Create new, name it Critical Element, and click Create.
  4. Click inside the signature editor and paste (Cmd+V or Ctrl+V). The logo, the orange rule and your details should appear.
  5. Under Signature defaults, choose Critical Element for both For new emails use and On reply/forward use.
  6. Scroll to the bottom and click Save Changes. Send yourself a test email to confirm.

The Gmail mobile apps use their own plain-text signature and can't show the logo. On your phone, set a text version: your name, title, and criticalelement.io.
